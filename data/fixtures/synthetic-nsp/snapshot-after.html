Post Matric Scholarships Scheme for SC Students

Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment

URGENT NOTICE: The last date for submission of online application has been extended up to 30-11-2026.

Institute verification deadline has been revised to 15-12-2026.

Eligibility Criteria

Applicable for students pursuing post-matric courses (UG and PG).

Annual family income should not exceed ₹2,50,000 per annum from all sources.

Candidate must belong to Scheduled Caste (SC) category.

Required Documents

  • Caste Certificate issued by competent authority
  • Income Certificate
  • Bonafide Student Certificate
  • Bank Passbook copy showing Aadhaar seeded account
